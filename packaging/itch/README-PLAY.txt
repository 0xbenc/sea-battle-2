SEA BATTLE 2

Extract the whole package before playing.
Linux: Play.sh, or ./sea-battle from a terminal.
macOS: Sea Battle 2.app or Play.command opens Terminal.
Windows: itch Play opens a console; browser downloads use Play.cmd.

Use a terminal at least 80 columns by 24 rows. Zoom out or enlarge it if prompted.
Choose Single player, or Host/Join to play with a friend. The host shares a private
invitation. Join reads the clipboard or connects as soon as you paste it.
Public multiplayer may download a verified connection helper; solo stays local.

These launchers do not install anything or change PATH.
See PLAYING.md for controls, settings, invitations and platform notes.
